# Mandela Motion Streetwear

CREATE A COMPLETE PREMIUM WEBSITE FOR MANDELA MOTION

Build a fully functional, visually stunning, modern streetwear fashion website for MANDELA MOTION, a South African clothing brand.

The website must look like an internationally recognised fashion label, combining African street culture, luxury fashion, underground creativity and futuristic design.

1. VISUAL DESIGN

Create a distinctive visual identity using:

Deep black, charcoal and metallic grey.

Silver highlights and subtle vibrant accents.

Premium typography and oversized editorial headlines.

Cinematic backgrounds and immersive 3D effects.

Smooth animations and interactive transitions.

Parallax scrolling and animated image reveals.

Beautiful product hover effects.

Modern glass effects used selectively.

Make the website vibrant and alive, not flat or boring.

2. HOMEPAGE

Create an unforgettable landing page featuring:

Large MANDELA MOTION branding.

A cinematic hero section.

Full-screen brand photography.

Animated headline and introduction.

A prominent SHOP COLLECTION button.

Featured products.

Latest drops.

A preview of the brand story.

Instagram/social media integration.

3. BRAND STORY

Create a dedicated section called OUR STORY.

Include a place for the original Mandela Motion background story that I will provide.

Preserve the original story and present it through beautiful typography, photography, animations and editorial layouts.

Do not invent the brand's history or replace its real background story with generic text.

4. CLOTHING COLLECTION

Create an interactive online store featuring:

LONG LIVE DR Sleeveless Shirt

LONG LIVE DR Hoodie

MM Tour Trucker Caps

NO FREE RIDE T-Shirts

Use my uploaded product photographs as the actual product images.

Each product must have:

Multiple photographs where available.

Product descriptions.

Editable prices.

Size selection.

Colour selection where applicable.

Add-to-cart functionality.

Product detail pages.

WhatsApp ordering.

5. SHOPPING EXPERIENCE

Build a functional shopping cart.

Customers must be able to:

Browse collections.

Select clothing sizes.

Choose quantities.

Add and remove products.

View their cart.

See their order summary.

Proceed to checkout.

6. WHATSAPP INTEGRATION

Add WhatsApp ordering throughout the website.

When a customer checks out, automatically generate a WhatsApp message containing:

Product names.

Selected sizes.

Quantities.

Total order amount.

Customer details.

Use a configurable WhatsApp business number.

7. EMAIL INTEGRATION

Create a professional contact page with:

Business email address.

Working contact form.

Customer enquiry form.

Newsletter subscription.

Form submission confirmation.

Connect forms to a real email or form-submission service. Do not create buttons that look functional but do nothing.

8. INTERACTIVE FEATURES

Include:

3D animated elements.

Interactive product displays.

Smooth scrolling.

Animated navigation.

Custom page transitions.

Dynamic backgrounds.

Image hover interactions.

Mobile-friendly navigation.

Loading animations.

Keep animations smooth and optimized.

9. SOCIAL MEDIA

Create an Instagram section showcasing brand photography and updates.

Include links to the brand's social platforms, with placeholders where actual links are not yet provided.

10. FOOTER

Include:

MANDELA MOTION branding.

Shop.

Our Story.

Contact.

Instagram.

WhatsApp.

Email.

Newsletter.

Copyright information.

11. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

Fully responsive website.

Desktop and mobile optimization.

Functional shopping cart.

Working WhatsApp checkout.

Real email integration.

SEO optimization.

Fast loading.

Secure form handling.

Clean and maintainable code.

Functional navigation and buttons.

FINAL INSTRUCTION

Do not create a generic clothing website.

Create an unforgettable digital experience that makes MANDELA MOTION feel like a premium global streetwear brand with authentic South African roots.

Use my uploaded photographs, preserve the original brand story, and make the website visually distinctive.

Prioritize creativity, originality, functionality and premium presentation.

The finished website must be ready to publish, with clearly identified placeholders for information I still need to supply.
